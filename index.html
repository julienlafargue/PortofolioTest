About Me

A creative with a story to tell

My name is Tia-Lana and I'm a UK-born creative based in Paris. I have a deep love for visual storytelling which allows me to explore emotion, narrative and aesthetics through photography and media.

I enjoy experimenting with light, atmosphere and composition to create visuals that feel emotional, cinematic and personal. My work combines creativity with storytelling to create images that leave a lasting impression.
